The Problem

Most hospital software is built around departments, not patients

Tier-2 and Tier-3 hospitals run registration, labs, pharmacy, and billing on disconnected tools — leading to duplicate records, billing errors, and a fragmented patient experience.

Disconnected Systems

Separate software for OPD, lab, pharmacy and billing creates duplicate data entry and mismatched patient records.

Billing Errors & Leakage

Manual cross-referencing between departments causes missed charges, delayed invoices, and revenue leakage.

Slow Patient Throughput

Long wait times at every handoff point — registration to doctor, doctor to lab, lab to pharmacy — reduce patient satisfaction and capacity.

Our Approach

One patient ID.
Every department connected.

HMS is built on a single Unique Health ID (UHID) that follows the patient across every touchpoint. Every prescription, lab result, invoice, and follow-up is linked to one record — no duplication, no data loss.

  • Workflow-first design — the system is built around how patients actually move through your hospital, not around software modules.
  • Built on secure cloud infrastructure — backed by We2 Systems' enterprise platform engineering and data protection capabilities.
  • Designed for Tier-2 & Tier-3 hospitals — right-sized for 50–500 bed facilities without the complexity of enterprise-only platforms.

Flagship Deployment · Project PranaLink

Built by We2 Systems

HMS is the platform behind Project PranaLink — connected care for Bharat.

Prana means life force; Link represents the connection between patients, local clinics, hospitals, doctors, therapists, diagnostics and pharmacies. Project PranaLink is We2 Systems Pvt Ltd's AI-assisted healthcare access platform for small and mid-sized hospitals across Tier-2, Tier-3, semi-urban and rural India — built on the HMS platform.

The mission is to make quality healthcare more accessible, affordable and organised for patients who currently travel long distances for basic consultation, follow-up and chronic-care guidance.

One UHID, one care journey — registration, consultation, lab, pharmacy, billing and follow-up unified for every patient.
Built for underserved Bharat — small and mid-sized hospitals, nursing homes and clinics in Tier-2, Tier-3 and rural markets still running on manual registers and disconnected billing.
Roadmap toward AI-assisted care — remote triage, therapy follow-up and chronic-care monitoring layered on top of the core HMS workflow engine.
